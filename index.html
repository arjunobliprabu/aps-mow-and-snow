Service Provider Agreement

Terms and Conditions

Please read before signing up as a service provider.

1. Payment split. For every job you complete, 15% goes to TeenTastic Turf (admin) and you keep 85%. Payouts are sent manually via Venmo, typically within 10 days of completing a job.

2. Doing the work. You're responsible for showing up on time, completing each job to a professional standard, and letting us know right away if a booking has a problem.

3. Scheduling. TeenTastic Turf may reassign or cancel a job if it can't be completed as scheduled.

4. No guaranteed work. Signing up doesn't guarantee a minimum number of jobs or a minimum amount of income.

5. Leaving any time. You can stop working with TeenTastic Turf whenever you want, and TeenTastic Turf can remove your access whenever it needs to.
